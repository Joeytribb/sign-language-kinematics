Kinematic Synthesizer

Welcome to the Kinematic Synthesizer! Please note that this is a very simple, lightweight prototype implementation specifically built to test and de-risk our theoretical proposal. Final production systems will be significantly more sophisticated, expressive, and robust.

This prototype demonstrates a deterministic forward-kinematics and biomechanically constrained foundation for real-time sign language synthesis, directly implementing the phonological sublexicon inventory in ASL-LEX 2.0. Rather than treating sign language as an unconstrained black-box pixel prediction problem, our framework grounds generation in 15-DOF anatomical finger joints, 2-point deterministic contact trajectories (Approach P1 → Contact P2), continuous 3D spatial loci, and continuous volumetric collision avoidance.

🚀 Interactive Guide • How to Test the Engine:
  • Select & Synthesize Signs: Click any word in the right sidebar (e.g., WATER, YES, DEAF, MOTHER) to synthesize the sign and observe the 2-point approach and impact kinematics.
  • Explore 3D Spatial Loci: The 24 floating spheres represent anatomical landmarks and conversational signing spaces. Click and drag the master waypoint or hand to evaluate real-time inverse kinematics and humeral swivel!
  • Isolate Hand & Arms: Use the floating bottom deck to toggle the Body Figure off if you want to inspect the articulated skeleton hand and arms in complete isolation.
  • Live Kinematic Telemetry: Observe real-time approach vs contact waypoint coordinates (Approach P1 → Target P2), stroke distance, and impact dwell phases.
  • One-Hand Focus: Active signs currently feature 1-handed articulation. Full two-handed bilateral coordination support will be enabled in the upcoming update.
