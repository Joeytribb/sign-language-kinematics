### Pillar 1: The Commercial & Regulatory Forcing Function *(Why Enterprise Customers MUST Buy Today)*

#### 1. Statutory Federal Compliance Mandate (US DOT 14 CFR § 382.53)
Under the US Department of Transportation’s **Air Carrier Access Act (14 CFR § 382.53 / 14 CFR § 382.51)**, commercial airlines and airport authorities face severe federal consent decrees and six-figure statutory penalties if dynamic gate changes, delays, and emergency verbal PA broadcasts are not visually accessible to deaf travelers. Purchasing SAMMY is not an elective CSR/charity initiative—it is a mandatory legal compliance expenditure.

#### 2. The "Text Captions Fail" Scientific Proof (NIH / Gallaudet Research)
Over **70% of prelingually Deaf individuals read English below a 4th-grade reading level** (*Traxler, Journal of Deaf Studies and Deaf Education / Gallaudet Research Institute*). Sign language is an independent visual-spatial language with its own phonology and syntax—not word-by-word English speech. Dense, rapidly scrolling text tickers cause cognitive overload and functional failure during high-stress flight cancellations. Native 3D Sign Language is the only legally compliant visual equivalent.

#### 3. Pre-Approved Enterprise Budgets (The $20M+ Ava Precedent)
Ava (`ava.me`) raised $20M+ selling English speech-to-text live captions to **Air France, UCLA, Autodesk, and state agencies**, definitively proving to investors that enterprise and public-sector accessibility budgets are already approved and recurring. However, Ava only provides English text subtitles—leaving enterprises completely exposed to non-compliance for native signers. SAMMY does not need to educate procurement departments; we capture pre-existing budget lines with the 3D spatial signing engine they legally require.

#### 4. Zero Hardware CapEx Deployment ($0 Installation Cost)
Airports and municipal transit hubs do not need to buy specialized kiosks, proprietary cameras, or expensive displays. SAMMY is a lightweight software layer that deploys directly onto pre-existing Flight Information Display Systems (FIDS) monitors and boarding gate screens via standard browser runtimes. Zero hardware CapEx accelerates enterprise sales cycles from 18 months down to weeks.

#### 10. True Two-Way Dialogue (Forward + Backward)
Every competitor in the market is strictly one-way (speech-to-text captions or one-way avatar playback). SAMMY enables complete bidirectional communication:
1. **Hearing to Deaf:** Hearing staff speak normally; SAMMY renders a continuous 3D signing avatar on screen.
2. **Deaf to Hearing:** A deaf passenger signs back into a standard webcam; SAMMY translates 3D skeletal movements into spoken voice and clear text.

#### 11. Solving "Spatial State Collapse" via Causal Spatial Memory ($M_t$)
