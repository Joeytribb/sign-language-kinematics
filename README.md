### 1. Universal Design (The Curb-Cut Effect)
Just as sidewalk curb cuts originally designed for wheelchair users now empower parents with strollers and delivery workers, SAMMY’s visual communication architecture benefits broader populations:
- **70 Million Deaf individuals globally** who communicate primarily through 3D spatial sign languages.
- **40+ Million hard-of-hearing elderly travelers** who cannot decipher garbled public acoustic announcements in noisy terminal environments.
- **Neurodivergent & Dyslexic passengers** who face severe cognitive choke points when reading rapidly flipping text-only flight departure boards.

### 2. Deaf-Led Economic Mobility ("Deaf-in-the-Loop")
SAMMY does not use ungrounded AI to replace humans. Our platform creates a high-skill, remote employment pipeline:
- Certified Deaf native linguists, 3D animators, and ASL educators serve as **paid phonetic curators and quality-assurance engineers** who audit, validate, and govern the lexicon database.
- Eliminates cultural backlash, guarantees grammatical authenticity, and directly closes the disability wealth gap.

### 3. Strategic Synergy with Sorenson Communications (VRS)
SAMMY is architected to **protect and expand Sorenson's core Video Relay Service (VRS)** ecosystem:
- **Automates the Unstaffed 95%:** Handles routine gate reassignments, directional wayfinding, and boarding group calls where certified human interpreters are not physically stationed.
- **Instant Escalation to Sorenson VRS:** Features a one-touch emergency bridge connecting passengers directly to RID-certified Sorenson VRS human interpreters for complex exceptions (lost baggage, missed flights, medical distress), funneling high-margin relay minutes directly into Sorenson's network.

## 🔬 Core Technological Breakthrough: Why Video AI Fails

General-purpose generative video models (Sora, Runway, Kling) fail catastrophically at sign language:
1. **The Occlusion Crisis:** Hands crossing over the face or over the contralateral hand cause pixel-based models to hallucinate melting knuckles and morphing fingers.
2. **Phonetic Intolerance for Error:** A 2-inch locus shift changes *Mother* (chin) to *Father* (forehead); a 10-degree wrist pitch rotation alters grammatical aspect.
3. **Temporal Smearing:** High-speed fingerspelling (15+ micro-articulations/sec) is smoothed away by temporal autoencoders.

**SAMMY's Decoupled Two-Layer Architecture:**
