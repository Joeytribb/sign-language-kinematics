Flaw 01 • Community 🧤

Student "AI Gloves" & Toys

Well-meaning engineering students repeatedly invent sensor gloves identifying 20 isolated signs. The Deaf community fiercely rejects them: natural sign language is conveyed primarily through facial non-manual markers, spatial syntax, and torso posture—not isolated hand flexions.

Flaw 02 • Unit Economics 🎬

Optical Mocap Avatars

Incumbents pay $100K+ recording human signers in optical mocap suits, locking them to narrow, rigid pre-recorded clips. They cannot scale to open-domain vocabulary, and they burn expensive cloud GPUs streaming heavy MP4 video to screens—running up unsustainable bandwidth bills.

Flaw 03 • Hallucination 🌀

Generative Video Diffusion

Big Tech (Google, Meta) treats sign language as a 2D pixel problem. Video diffusion models suffer from catastrophic topological hallucinations: phantom 6th fingers, rubber-band bone stretching, $0.20/query compute fees, and 20-second latencies that make dynamic transit announcements impossible.
