Core Scientific Principles

01 / Macro vs Micro

The "Brain vs. Muscles" Principle

High-level linguistic discourse is planned at 1–2 Hz by Spatial-SignLLM, while continuous musculoskeletal execution is handled by deterministic 15-DOF IK and manifold residual diffusion at 60 Hz.

02 / Manifold Geometry

Manifold-Projected Diffusion

Residual noise diffusion operates on rigid kinematic scaffolds with holonomic SHAKE/RATTLE projections, mathematically guaranteeing zero bone stretching and preventing rubbery artifacts.

03 / Discourse Memory

Decoupled Causal Spatial Memory

An external \(O(1)\) stateful register tracks active 3D referent loci across unlimited dialogue turns, applying deictic parity for 180° perspective invariance across signing interlocutors.
