An Autonomous Bidirectional Communication Digital Twin for Continuous Sign Language AI.

Bridging Deaf signers and hearing professionals through real-time cross-modal dialogue (<100 ms latency). In critical healthcare and civic consultations, the digital twin interprets continuous signing video into fluent spoken discourse, while synthesizing physically authentic, grammatically verified 3D sign language animation from spoken discourse.

Over 70 million Deaf individuals communicate primarily through natural sign languages. Because sign languages possess no conventional written form, contemporary machine learning faces critical representational bottlenecks: unconstrained generative models suffer from non-physiological bone stretching and joint dislocations that smear centimeter-scale minimal pairs, while streaming translators suffer from spatial state collapse under truncated temporal windows. We resolve these limitations through a unified tripartite foundation architecture: (1) an Articulatory Perception Channel crawling web-scale continuous video to discretize signing into structured linguistic tokens; (2) an Adapted Multimodal Foundation Core (Llama 3.1 8B on EuroHPC LUMI) internalizing 3D spatial grammar and causal memory; and (3) an Embodied Biomechanical Actuator compiling token streams into continuous 60 Hz 3D avatar motion under strict anatomical and strain constraints.

Tripartite Foundation Architecture

Perception Channel, Foundation Reasoning, and Embodied Actuation

01 / Synthesis Channel • Universal Parametric Generator @ 60 Hz

40-DOF Universal Sign Generator & Lexicon Studio

Synthesizes any sign across a 629,760 phonetic space from 5 foundational parametric lexicons (41 handshapes, 24 3D loci, 10 orientations, 16 movements, 4 bilateral modes) covering all 2,723 signs in the ASL-LEX dataset. Closed-form 2-bone arm IK, multi-pass inter-finger anti-penetration, and continuous volumetric collision barriers eliminate non-physiological bone deformation and minimal-pair smearing.

Minimal Finger Collisions • 26/26 Alphabet PASS • 50/50 ASL-LEX PASS • 60 Hz WebGL

02 / Reasoning Core • Foundation Model

BiSign-LLM (3D Spatial Syntax & Cross-Modal Dialogue)

Adapts pre-trained open-weight frontier models (Llama 3.1 8B) via spatial-lexical vocabulary expansion on EuroHPC LUMI. Maintains persistent discourse referents and causal spatial memory (Mt) immune to sliding-window truncation.

03 / Perception Channel • Community Co-Design

Articulatory Tokenizer & Participatory Co-Design

Ingests in-the-wild signing video at scale, tracking articulated hand kinematics, 50 facial blendshapes, and 3D gaze rays into discrete linguistic tokens. Developed in direct partnership with the Finnish Association of the Deaf (Kuurojen Liitto ry) under Finland's Sign Language Act (359/2015).
